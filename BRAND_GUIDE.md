# ROI Resources — Brand System

## Brand idea

ROI Resources backs exceptional people with the structure and freedom to build their own future.

## Logo

The new mark is a geometric **R** with a forward path in brass. The R represents **Rohaizat**, the family legacy behind ROI. The forward path expresses ambition, movement and the freedom to go further.

- Use the full horizontal logo for formal documents, proposals and wide layouts.
- Use the monogram for website navigation, social avatars, sponsorship emblems and small applications.
- Prefer a single-colour main mark with the brass path as the only accent.
- Never stretch, rotate, outline or add gradients to the mark.

## Core colours

| Name | Hex | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Carbon | `#091411` | Primary dark background and wordmark |
| Heritage Emerald | `#0E624D` | Institutional brand colour |
| Forward Mint | `#71C7A2` | Controlled highlight and digital interaction |
| Legacy Brass | `#B58A4A` | Family legacy and forward-path accent |
| Warm Bone | `#F4F1E8` | Primary light background |
| Soft Sage | `#A8B4AE` | Secondary text and supporting UI |

## Typography

- Display: Space Grotesk, medium to bold.
- Body: Manrope, regular to bold.
- Use sentence case for body copy and compact uppercase labels for navigation or section indexing.

## Brand tone

Bold, disciplined, human and ambitious. ROI should feel like a serious platform behind uncommon people—not a generic corporate group or a loud technology startup.
